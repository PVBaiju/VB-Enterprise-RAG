# Employee Handbook

## Leave Policy

### Annual Leave
- Full-time employees: 20 days per year
- Part-time employees: Pro-rated based on hours
- Carryover: Maximum 5 days to next year
- Payout: Unused leave paid out on separation

### Sick Leave
- 10 days per year
- Does not carry over
- Medical certificate required for absences exceeding 3 days

### Parental Leave
- Maternity: 16 weeks
- Paternity: 4 weeks
- Adoption: 12 weeks

### Other Leave
- Compassionate leave: Up to 5 days
- Emergency leave: As per company policy
- Jury duty: As required by law

## Reimbursement Rules

### Travel Expenses
- Flights: Economy class for flights under 6 hours
- Hotels: 3-star standard or equivalent
- Daily allowance: $50 per day for meals
- Car rental: Compact or mid-size only
- Mileage: $0.58 per mile for personal vehicles

### Meal Allowances
- Breakfast: $15
- Lunch: $20
- Dinner: $30
- Alcohol: Maximum $10 per meal

### Equipment
- Laptop: Up to $1500
- Monitor: Up to $400
- Desk furniture: Up to $500
- Software: Pre-approval required

## Onboarding Process

### Week 1
1. HR orientation and compliance training
2. IT setup (laptop, accounts, email)
3. Office tour and badge access
4. Meet your team

### Week 2
1. Department training
2. Safety and security briefing
3. System access verification
4. First project assignment

### Week 3-4
1. Shadowing senior team members
2. Initial task assignments
3. Feedback session with manager
4. Probation period begins

## Working Hours

### Standard Schedule
- Monday-Friday: 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM
- Lunch break: 1 hour (12:00 PM - 1:00 PM)
- Total: 8 hours per day

### Flexibility
- Flexible start time: 8:00 AM - 10:00 AM
- Flexible end time: 5:00 PM - 7:00 PM
- Remote work: Up to 2 days per week with manager approval
- Core hours: 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM (must be present)

### Overtime
- Overtime compensation: 1.5x hourly rate
- Comp time: 1.5 hours off for each hour worked
- Approval required: Manager sign-off mandatory

## Code of Conduct

### Professional Behavior
- Treat all employees with respect
- Maintain professional communication
- Avoid conflicts of interest
- Preserve confidentiality

### Workplace Safety
- Report all incidents immediately
- Follow safety procedures
- Do not operate equipment without training
- Maintain clean and safe workspace

### Performance Standards
- Meet project deadlines
- Maintain quality standards
- Provide constructive feedback
- Continuous learning and development

## Benefits

### Health Insurance
- Medical: 80% coverage
- Dental: 50% coverage
- Vision: 50% coverage
- Mental health: Covered up to 10 sessions/year

### Financial Benefits
- 401(k): 5% employer match
- Stock options: Vesting over 4 years
- Bonus: Performance-based, up to 20%

### Wellness
- Gym membership: $50/month allowance
- Wellness programs: Free access
- Mental health support: EAP available
- Flexible work arrangements
